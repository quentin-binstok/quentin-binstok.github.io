I'm currently a second year master's student in engineering physics at the University of Liège, Belgium. My interests include numerical methods and computational physics, especially for continuum mechanics.

My past projects include:
- a particle-in-cell code for inviscid newtonian fluids, for both internal and free-surface flows, available at [https://github.com/quentin-binstok/PIC](https://github.com/quentin-binstok/PIC),
- a FDTD code for the simulation of electromagnetic waves, with an emphasis on high-performance computing (OpenMP, MPI, CUDA), available at [https://github.com/Aerozine/EM-Wave](https://github.com/Aerozine/EM-Wave),
- experimental research on transparent conducting materials, more specifically on TiO2 coatings on silver nanowire networks, under the supervision of [Pr. Ngoc Duy Nguyen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=KIlUQZMAAAAJ&hl=en&inst=10437135047913140093).

My master's thesis will be linked to the PFEM2 method, under the supervision of [Pr. Jean-Philippe Ponthot](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dwFpwXYAAAAJ&hl=en&inst=10437135047913140093) and [Pr. Romain Boman](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=P6zCaCkAAAAJ&inst=10437135047913140093).
