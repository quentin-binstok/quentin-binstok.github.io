I contributed to this research in the context of the class _Personal experimental project_ ([APRI0006](https://www.programmes.uliege.be/cocoon/20262027/cours/APRI0006-1.html)) at the University of Liège. I was in [Pr. Ngoc Duy Nguyen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=KIlUQZMAAAAJ&hl=en&inst=10437135047913140093)'s team, the [SPIN](https://www.spin.uliege.be/cms/c_6792399/en/spin) lab, and under the supervision of [Amaury Baret](https://abaret-phys.github.io/). He is the one who created this poster. I contributed with lab work, mainly consisting in the manufacturing of the samples, and a few measurements.

The skills I acquired with this project include spin coating, preparation of precursor solutions, spectrophotometry, the writing of research proposals, and data processing.
