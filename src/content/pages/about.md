I'm currently a second year master's student in engineering physics at the University of Liège, Belgium. My interests are numerical methods and computational physics, especially for continuum mechanics and CFD.

My past projects include

- a particle-in-cell code for inviscid fluids, for both internal and free-surface flows, which you can read more about [in the portfolio](/portfolio/1_PIC),
- a [FDTD solver](/portfolio/2_HPC) for the simulation of electromagnetic waves, with an emphasis on high-performance computing (OpenMP, MPI, CUDA),
- experimental research on transparent conducting materials, more specifically on $\mathrm{TiO}_2$ coatings on silver nanowire networks, under the supervision of [Pr. Ngoc Duy Nguyen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=KIlUQZMAAAAJ&hl=en&inst=10437135047913140093).


My master's thesis is on the PFEM-2 method, under the supervision of [Pr. Jean-Philippe Ponthot](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dwFpwXYAAAAJ&hl=en&inst=10437135047913140093) and [Pr. Romain Boman](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=P6zCaCkAAAAJ&inst=10437135047913140093). My main interests are error estimation, compute performance, and a comparison with other semi-Lagrangian methods, such as particle-in-cell methods.
